# Living TV Support

Living TV turns your Apple TV into a window on a quiet forest: a calm, looping scene with soft music and the occasional wildlife visitor. You can also add your own M3U playlist to watch other channels.

## Contact

Questions, problems or suggestions: email **support@betterlivingworkshop.com**. We usually reply within a few days.

## Getting started

- The Forest is channel 0. It plays as soon as the app opens.
- Press **Up** or **Down** on the Siri Remote to change channels, and click to open the guide.
- **Play/Pause** mutes the Forest's music or pauses a channel.

## Adding your own playlist

Living TV doesn't come with any channels besides the Forest. To watch others, add a playlist you already have:

1. Click to open the guide, then choose **Settings**.
2. Choose **My Playlists**, then **Add Your Own Playlist**.
3. Enter the playlist's link (it must start with `https://`). M3U and M3U8 playlists work. The guide link is optional: leave it blank and Living TV will try to find "now and next" listings for your channels on its own. If your playlist provider gives you an XMLTV guide link (`.xml` or `.xml.gz`), you can add it too.

Typing a long link is easier with dictation or the keyboard on your iPhone.

Playlists are checked for updates once a week. In **My Playlists** you can turn a playlist off or remove it at any time.

### Where to find playlists

Many broadcasters and radio stations list a live stream or M3U link on their own websites. Some TV and internet providers offer an M3U link for your account. Community-maintained lists such as the iptv-org project on GitHub collect links to publicly available streams. Only add playlists you have the right to watch where you live.

## Common questions

**A channel won't play.** Streams in third-party playlists change or stop without notice. Living TV skips channels that fail and checks them again later. If a whole playlist stops working, check the link with whoever publishes it.

**The guide is empty for some channels.** Listings come from the XMLTV link you add, or from public guide services when a channel's ID matches. Not every channel has listings.

**The family filter hid a channel I want.** The family filter is on by default and hides channels whose names or groups match a list of adult and premium terms. You can turn it off in Settings.

**After a while it goes back to the Forest.** After 10 minutes with nothing playing, the app returns to the Forest.
