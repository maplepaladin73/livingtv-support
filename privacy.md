# Living TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Living TV does not collect any data about you.

- No accounts, sign-in or registration.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No data is sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is sent to us.

## What stays on your Apple TV

Your settings (family filter, channel groups), the playlist and guide links you add, and cached copies of channel lists, guide listings and channel logos are stored only on your Apple TV. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network connections

The Forest plays from files inside the app and needs no connection.

When you add a playlist, your Apple TV connects directly to the servers involved: the address of the playlist and guide you entered, the servers that host each channel's stream, and, to fill in the guide and channel logos for your playlist's channels, the public services epg.pw, i.mjh.nz and the iptv-org logo list on GitHub. Only channel IDs are requested; nothing about you is sent. As with any internet connection, those servers can see your device's IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. Living TV does not run any servers and never receives this information.

## Children

Living TV collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The family filter is on by default.

## Disclaimer

Living TV does not provide, host or endorse any channels. Playlists you add are third-party content, used at your own discretion; the developer makes no representation about them and accepts no liability for them.
